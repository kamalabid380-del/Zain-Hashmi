1 The launch offer (your hook)

  • Simplest: Free delivery on orders over Rs. 3,000 (matches your shipping rule).
  • Stronger for first sales: 10% off your first order with code LAUNCH10.
Always lead with "Cash on Delivery — pay when it arrives." In Pakistan this removes the biggest fear of buying online.

3 Set up the channels (~1 hour)

  1. Instagram Business: create as @zainhashmi → convert to a Business account → paste the bio → add zainhashmi.com as the link → connect a Facebook Page (needed for ads later).
  2. WhatsApp Business (free app): set up profile → add a catalog of key products with prices → turn on greeting + away messages. Lets hesitant buyers order by chat.
  3. Facebook Page: match name/logo/bio → post the launch caption → share into Lahore buy/sell fashion groups.

4 Create the discount in Shopify

  1. Discounts → Create discount → Amount off products.
  2. Choose Percentage → 10%, code LAUNCH10.
  3. Applies to entire order (or launch collection) → once per customer → set active dates → Save.
  4. Test it: add to cart on the storefront, apply LAUNCH10, confirm 10% comes off.

6 Optional paid boost

  1. Meta Ads Manager → boost your best launch post.
  2. Audience: Pakistan / Lahore, age range, interests clothing / fashion / online shopping.
  3. Start small: Rs. 1,500–3,000/day. Objective: Traffic or Sales.
  4. Kill ads that don't bring orders after 3 days; scale what works.

7 Trust builders that convert COD buyers

  • Real product photos (not just supplier images) + a clear Size Guide.
  • State the exchange / return steps plainly.
  • Show WhatsApp ordering for people who don't trust checkout.
  • Post customer reviews / photos as soon as you get them.

8 Measure

Watch Shopify Analytics (sessions → orders) and which channel sends buyers. Double down on what works.

  • Instagram insights: reach, saves, profile visits, link clicks.
  • WhatsApp: how many chats turn into orders.
  • Shopify: conversion rate, top products, where traffic comes from.
